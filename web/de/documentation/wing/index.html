Der Flügel

Grundriss, Profil, Struktur.

Wing geometry is a single source of truth — an arbitrary outline, not bird-specific scalars.

Planform

A WingShape is a half-span plus an ordered list of spanwise stations. Each station carries leading-edge position, chord, twist and dihedral. Sweep is implicit — the slope of the leading-edge polyline — never a stored parameter.

Section

Every station carries a SectionProfile: rest camber, trailing-edge reflex, and membrane compliance. The thin-airfoil response shifts the zero-lift angle with camber and couples camber + reflex into a sectional pitching moment.

Structure

Every station carries a StructureProfile: bending stiffness EI, torsional stiffness GJ, and relaxation times. A root-clamped cantilever pass turns vertical force into bending moment → deflection/slope, and sectional pitching moment into torque → twist. A stiffer armwing and a flexible handwing yield realistic load-induced washout.

Aeroelastic loop

Deformation is not cosmetic. Aeroelastic twist adds to incidence; bending adds to flap position. The wing feeds its own loads back into its shape — the beating wing is a structure, not a picture.
