Physics

The reduced real-time model.

The simulation resolves a full vehicle at a fixed 240 Hz timestep: two wings, a body, and a tail.

Reference frames

  • Body — +x forward, +y right, +z up (Unreal's left-handed layout).
  • Wing local — x chordwise toward the trailing edge, y root→tip, z wing normal.

All units are SI internally; the Unreal boundary converts metres ↔ centimetres.

Discretisation

  • 16 spanwise strips per wing (Haskell simulates both wings; C++ OrniCore simulates one isolated wing as the research reference).
  • Local angle of attack, Reynolds number and separation state are computed per strip.

Forces

  • Lift — perpendicular to the local planar flow.
  • Drag — opposes the local planar flow; dissipative.
  • Separation — a continuous per-element state from 0 (attached) to 1 (separated), giving partial stall.
  • Added mass — the reaction of the air the wing accelerates.
  • LEV — leading-edge vortex lift captured in the stall model.
  • Crossflow — spanwise transport driven by sweep, gated by flapping phase.

Signs and constants are single-sourced in docs/physics-convention.md and asserted by regression tests.
