调校

把机翼调到你的手感。

Tuning is the martial art of the simulator — small movements, felt consequences.

Cadence

Throttle maps to stroke cadence (0.5…3.2 Hz) through the beat-locked oscillator. Find the cadence where the wing stops fighting itself.

Ferocity

Raise per-half ferocity for a sharper, more brutal stroke; lower it for a languid, elastic one. The wing's character changes before its lift does.

Mixer and PID

The mixer routes aileron into stroke asymmetry and elevator into tail deflection. PID holds attitude — but every gain is a commitment. Assists are controllers, always visible, always off-able.

Structure

Bend and twist relax toward their loaded shapes. Tune EI and GJ until the wing carries its own washout. The machine should feel alive under your thumbs, not bolted down.
