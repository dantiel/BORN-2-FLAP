01

Essence

An ornithopter is not a quad. It is a beating wing.

Where a drone hovers by force of motors, an ornithopter is the air it pushes. BORN2FLAP models the wing — its geometry, its stiffness, its camber, its stall — and lets the machine find flight on its own. Thrust, lift and control all emerge from one source: the wingbeat.

Real physics

Segmented wings, partial stall, added mass and aeroelastic bending — resolved at a fixed timestep, never faked.

Real control

CRSF radio, ONDAS mixers, PID and servo kernels ported from real firmware. Fly it with a real transmitter.

Real consequence

Sweep, washout, camber and stiffness change how it flies. Build it, then survive it.

02

The Wing

Aeroelastic, honest, alive.

Two wings, sixteen spanwise strips each. Local angle of attack, Reynolds number and separation state are computed per strip. Crossflow drifts along the span with sweep. The wing bends under load — torsional washout softens the tip, bending deepens the flap — and that deformation feeds straight back into incidence.

Wings 2 × 16 spanwise strips
Half-span 0.72 m
Chord 0.22 → 0.09 m shoulder → tip
Stall partial, dynamic, per-strip
Crossflow sweep-driven, spanwise transport
Structure bending EI + torsion GJ, relaxed
Waveform asymmetric dwell / pointed, beat-locked
Timestep fixed 240 Hz
03

Architecture

Three languages, one beating heart.

Haskell MathCore
The canonical physics — aerodynamics, kinematics, control and stabilisation. Pure, total, tested.
Unreal Engine
Rendering, Chaos rigid bodies, world interaction, cockpit, replay and game modes.
C / C++ bridge
The FFI boundary into Unreal, plus the tested OrniCore research fallback.
Ruby brain
App state, menus, rules and orchestration — the slow mind behind the fast math.

04

Gameplay

Light to begin, hard to master.

Free Flight

Open skies, thermal hunting, and the pure pleasure of staying aloft.

Flight School

Nine lessons from neutral trim to manual tuning — ghost sticks, corridors, replays.

Race

Gates, splits, ghosts and classes through canyons and cities.

Freestyle

Trick challenges judged by the wing, not the scoreboard.

Precision Landing

Spot landings in wind — the oldest discipline, the hardest.

Test Bench

Wind-tunnel view: force vectors, stall maps, live wing deformation.

Natural Valley

A forested valley with a winding river, firs, grass and moss — terrain and tree trunks you can actually collide with.

Real RC Transmitter

Plug in your own USB transmitter and fly on real sticks — no drivers, no translation layer. F3 opens calibration.
