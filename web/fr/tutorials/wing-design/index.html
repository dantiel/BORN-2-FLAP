Conception d'aile

La géométrie est le destin.

Every number you change is a decision the air will judge.

Chord

A long armwing chord and a tapered handwing give lift inboard and agility outboard. Flatten the tip and you lose roll authority; fatten it and you drag.

Sweep and washout

Sweep is the slope of the leading edge — it steers crossflow toward the tip. Washout (incidence dropping toward the tip) keeps the tip flying after the root stalls. Both are emergent, not decorative.

Camber and reflex

Camber buys lift and shifts the zero-lift angle. Trailing-edge reflex shifts the pitching moment — a tail that trims without a tail.

Stiffness

A stiff armwing and a flexible handwing load-bend into washout. Too stiff and it is a plank; too soft and it folds. Structure is a flight parameter, not a detail.
