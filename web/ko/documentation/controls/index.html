Control runs through the same path as real firmware.

Channels

CRSF channels arrive raw (172…1811). Aileron, elevator, throttle, rudder, arm, frequency and profile are normalised and fed to the mixer.

Flapping hysteresis

Flapping engages when throttle exceeds the threshold and releases only after it drops by a hysteresis band — so the wing does not chatter at the edge of idle.

Steering

Aileron command becomes an asymmetric stroke; elevator becomes tail deflection; rudder shifts the flap amplitude left versus right. Gliding keeps full wing authority.

Mixer and PID

The ONDAS servo mixer and PID loops are ported from the firmware, so in-simulator behaviour tracks the hardware it mirrors.
