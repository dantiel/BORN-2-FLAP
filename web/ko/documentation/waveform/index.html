The flapping waveform is a faithful port of the PteronautOS oscillator — not a sine.

The oscillator

An OscillatorState advances a debt-pendulum phase whose phaseOffset is attracted to whole strokes by -ω0² · sin(φ). It beat-locks to whole flapping cycles, so cadence and phasing stay coherent under load.

The shape

shapeWave produces an asymmetric dwell/pointed wave: per-half ferocity (0…8) sets how much of each half-stroke is dwell versus thrust. A centre-skew parameter shifts the point of reversal. The result is the hard down-stroke and soft recovery of a real wingbeat.

Ferocity

Ferocity is the texture of the stroke — low values are languid and elastic, high values are sharp and brutal. Tune it to match the animal you are building.
