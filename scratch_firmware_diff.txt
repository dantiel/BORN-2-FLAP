+-- | Glide: slow soar-pump stroke with differential-amplitude roll/yaw.

+-- the right mechanism for a static trim — but the wings never hold a static
+-- roll tilt. Aileron and rudder grow/shrink the L/R pump amplitude instead
+-- (aileron → roll couple, rudder → yaw), exactly like the flapping branch's
+-- amplitude differentials; the flap velocity of the stroke is what makes them
+-- aerodynamic. Positive aileron pumps the LEFT wing harder, lifting that side
+-- into a right bank (stick-right convention, proverse yaw into the turn).
